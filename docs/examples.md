# Examples

These notebooks live in the [`examples`](https://github.com/meyer-nils/fiberoripy/tree/master/examples)
directory and are executed when the documentation is built, so the figures come from
the version of fiberoripy documented here. Run them interactively on
[Binder](https://mybinder.org/v2/gh/meyer-nils/fiberoripy/HEAD).

## Orientation models

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   [__Model comparison in simple shear__](examples/orientation/comparison_perfectshear.html)

    Reproduces Figure 2 of Favaloro & Tucker III, *Composites Part A* 126 (2019).

-   [__ARD-RSC in simple shear__](examples/orientation/ardrsc_perfectshear.html)

    Re-orientation predicted by the ARD-RSC model in a perfect shear flow.

-   [__ARD-RSC in a rotating flow__](examples/orientation/ardrsc_perfectrotation.html)

    Re-orientation predicted by the ARD-RSC model in a perfectly rotating flow.

-   [__iARD in simple shear__](examples/orientation/iard_perfectshear.html)

    Re-orientation predicted by the iARD model in a perfect shear flow.

-   [__RSC under combined loading__](examples/orientation/rsc_combiload.html)

    Re-orientation predicted by the RSC model under shear and elongation.

-   [__Orthotropic fitted closures__](examples/orientation/orthofitclosures_perfectshear.html)

    ORF, ORW and ORW3 compared in a perfect shear flow.

</div>
